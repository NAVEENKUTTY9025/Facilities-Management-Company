Healthcare Estates

Infection-Control Led

In clinical environments, every process is a patient-safety process. Our healthcare teams are trained in infection prevention, HTM compliance, and cleanroom protocols — with audit trails that stand up to inspection.

  • HTM 04-01 / 06-01 compliance support
  • Enhanced cleaning and infection-control audits
  • Medical gas and critical-system PPM
Industrial & Logistics

Uptime Is Everything

Production lines and distribution centres can't afford downtime. We combine predictive maintenance, condition monitoring, and rapid reactive cover to keep your assets running at maximum availability.

  • Predictive and condition-based maintenance
  • Critical spares management
  • 24/7 engineering cover

Safe, Clean Learning Spaces

Schools, colleges, and universities need FM that works around term calendars and safeguarding requirements. We deliver deep cleans between terms, reactive cover during exams, and compliant water and fire safety all year.

  • Term-time and holiday deep-clean programmes
  • DBS-checked staff and safeguarding compliance
  • Legionella and fire-safety management
