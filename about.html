Our Story

From One Building to 500+ Sites

Facilitas began in 2008 as a small maintenance crew servicing a single commercial tower in Chicago. We noticed that building owners were juggling dozens of vendors — cleaners, electricians, security firms — with no single view of performance.

So we built a different model: one integrated team, one contract, and one dashboard. Today we manage hard and soft FM services across commercial offices, hospitals, warehouses, and mixed-use developments — always with the same obsession for reliability and transparency.

We are ISO 41001 certified, fully insured, and proud to be a certified minority-owned business.
