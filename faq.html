Common Questions

We deliver integrated facilities management across hard FM (M&E maintenance, HVAC, electrical, plumbing, PPM), soft FM (commercial cleaning, hygiene, waste, front-of-house), security & access control, and energy management. We can provide single services or a fully bundled FM contract.
Most new contracts mobilize within 10–15 working days. This includes TUPE consultation where applicable, site inductions, asset verification, and onboarding onto our CMMS and client dashboard. Urgent mobilizations can be accelerated on a case-by-case basis.
Pricing depends on scope, site size, service frequency, and contract length. We typically quote a fixed monthly fee for planned services plus agreed rates for reactive works. Every proposal includes a transparent pricing schedule with no hidden fees.
Yes. Facilitas is ISO 41001 (facilities management), ISO 45001 (health & safety), and ISO 14001 (environmental) certified. We carry full public and employers' liability insurance, and our teams are trained and vetted to industry standards.
Absolutely. Every client gets access to our client dashboard, where you can view SLA performance, PPM completion, reactive job status, compliance documents, and energy reports in real time. Admin users also have a separate dashboard for portfolio-level oversight.
Yes. Live contracts include access to our 24/7 emergency line with guaranteed response times — typically under 2 hours for critical issues. Emergency call-out rates are agreed in your contract schedule.
Standard contracts run for 12 months, but we offer 6-month pilot agreements for new clients and can accommodate longer terms with preferential pricing. We also support multi-year contracts with annual performance reviews.
Both. We manage single buildings and large multi-site portfolios across commercial, healthcare, industrial, and mixed-use sectors. Our regional structure means local teams with national oversight.
We maintain a live compliance register for every site covering fire safety, water hygiene (L8), asbestos, electrical safety, gas safety, and lift inspections. Certificates and remedial actions are tracked in the dashboard with automatic alerts before expiry.
Named client contacts receive role-based access. You can add multiple users (e.g. site managers, finance, compliance leads) with different permission levels. Admin dashboard access is reserved for portfolio-level and internal FM teams.
No hidden charges. Your proposal clearly separates fixed monthly fees, agreed reactive rates, and any optional extras. Consumables and materials are either included or billed at pre-agreed rates with prior approval.
Yes. We build flexibility into every agreement via a change-control process. Scope additions or reductions are documented, priced transparently, and reflected in your dashboard within one billing cycle.
